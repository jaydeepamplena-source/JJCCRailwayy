JJCC RAILWAYS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

JJCC Railways respects the privacy of its website users. This Privacy Policy explains what information the railway reservation website collects, how it is used, and how users can request deletion of their information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, JJCC Railways may process:

  • Account information, including email address and full name.
  • Profile information, such as phone number when provided.
  • Authentication information needed to identify an account.
  • Booking references and reservation records.
  • Train selection, departure and arrival stations, travel dates, booking status, and fare information.
  • Passenger information provided during booking, including passenger names, ages, passenger types, and assigned seats.
  • Basic profile and email information authorized through Google or Facebook sign-in, when used.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account access.
  • Process and display train reservations.
  • Manage passenger and seat information.
  • Display booking references, travel details, and status.
  • Respond to user support and data deletion requests.

3. Storage and Service Providers

JJCC Railways uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Information may also be processed by Google or Meta when users choose their respective sign-in options.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable service terms.

4. Information Sharing

User information is used for the operation of JJCC Railways and its reservation features. It may be processed by the service providers described above. It is not intended to be sold as a product or used for unrelated purposes.

5. Data Security

JJCC Railways uses authentication and database access controls to help protect account and reservation information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Account and reservation information may be retained while needed to provide the service, manage reservations, respond to requests, and address applicable operational or legal needs.

7. User Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and personal information by following the instructions on our Data Deletion page.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features or information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact the JJCC Railways project team using its designated public support email address.
