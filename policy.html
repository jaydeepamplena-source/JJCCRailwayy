Luggage & Travel Policy

Everything you need to know about travelling with JJCC Railways

  • Each passenger may bring one large bag (up to 23 kg) and one carry-on bag (up to 7 kg).
  • Keep your bags with you, or store them in the luggage racks at the end of each coach.
  • Extra bags may be carried subject to space and a small handling fee at the station.
  • Folded bicycles are carried free. Full-size bicycles need a reservation on services with bike space.
  • Sports equipment, musical instruments and similar items must fit in the luggage area without blocking aisles.
  • Items heavier than 30 kg or longer than 1.5 m may not be carried.
  • Carry a valid government-issued ID. The name on your ticket must match your ID.
  • Discounted fares (student, senior citizen, PWD) require the matching ID at the gate and on board.
  • Show your e-ticket on your phone or as a printout. Keep it until you leave the destination station.
  • Small pets travel free in a secure, ventilated carrier that stays on your lap or under your seat.
  • Guide and assistance dogs travel free and may sit with their handler on all services.
  • Pets may not occupy seats. Passengers are responsible for their animals at all times.
  • Request boarding assistance at least 24 hours before travel through Customer Service.
  • Wheelchair spaces and priority seats are available in designated coaches.
  • Station staff can help with boarding, alighting and transfers at all JJCC stations.
  • Be considerate: keep noise low, and give priority seats to those who need them.
  • Smoking and vaping are not allowed on trains or in enclosed station areas.
  • Dangerous or prohibited items are not allowed. Staff may ask passengers to leave if safety is at risk.
