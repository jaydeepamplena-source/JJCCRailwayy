Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions passengers ask most often.

01

Can I change my ticket?

Flexible fares can be changed without a fee before departure. Saver fares may require a fare difference and exchange fee.

02

What ID should I bring?

Bring one valid government-issued photo ID. Senior, PWD, and student discounts require the corresponding valid identification.

03

How do digital tickets work?

Show the QR code on your phone or a printed copy at the gate and during onboard inspection.
