JJCC RAILWAYS

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 3, 2026

JJCC Railways respects the privacy of its users. Users may request the deletion of their accounts and personal information associated with the railway reservation website.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send a data deletion request to the official JJCC Railways support email address.
  2. Use the subject line: JJCC Railways – Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your JJCC Railways account.
  4. Wait for the team to verify your request and provide further instructions.

2. Information That May Be Deleted

Depending on the request and applicable retention requirements, the following information may be included:

  • JJCC Railways user account and profile information.
  • Personal contact information stored in the account.
  • Associated passenger information.
  • Associated booking and reservation information.

3. Verification of Requests

The JJCC Railways team may verify the identity of the requester to make sure that personal information is not deleted through an unauthorized request.

4. Processing of Requests

After verification, the team will review and process the deletion request. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

5. Important Security Reminder

Never include your password, authentication tokens, or other login credentials in a data deletion request.
